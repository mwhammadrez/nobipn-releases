# Privacy Policy — nobipn

Last updated: November 2026

## No-Logs Commitment
- **nobipn** does **not** log, store, or inspect your browsing history, DNS queries, traffic destinations, or packet contents.
- Android's `VpnService` API is used exclusively to create an encrypted tunnel between your device and VPN servers when you tap Connect.

## Account Information (Optional VIP Accounts)
- Using the free tier requires **no registration**.
- If you choose to create an account (via Email or Sign in with Google) for VIP access, we store only your name, email address, subscription expiration status, and active session identifier to manage your VIP entitlement and enforce single-session security.

## Local Device Storage
- Fastest-server latency memory and language preferences are stored locally on your device and can be cleared anytime from Android app settings.

## Contact & Account Deletion
For privacy inquiries or account deletion requests, contact us via Telegram: [@mmdrlx](https://t.me/mmdrlx).
